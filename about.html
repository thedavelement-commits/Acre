About

A house built the slow way.

Acre began with one office, one advisor and a refusal to present a home nobody had stood inside.

01
We started in 2008

One office above a bakery in Ojai, one advisor, and a rule that has never changed: nobody presents a home they have not walked through themselves. It made us slower than the portals. It also made us right more often.

02
How we work

Every residence is checked again each week. Every buyer gets one advisor, not a queue. Every offer is explained in plain language before it is signed, including the parts that are not in your favour.

03
Where we are going

Nine states, thirty two advisors, and no ambition to become a portal. We would rather be the brokerage people call back than the one with the most listings.
