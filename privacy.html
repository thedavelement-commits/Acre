Privacy notice

What we collect when you use this site or enquire about a residence, why we hold it, and how to make us stop.

1. Who is responsible

Acre Residential is responsible for the information described here. Our address is on the contact page, and you can reach our privacy contact at privacy@acre.homes.

2. What we collect

The details you give us, such as your name, email, phone number and anything you write in an enquiry, along with the residences you viewed and the searches you ran.

3. Why we hold it

To answer your enquiry, arrange viewings, send residences if you asked for them and understand which parts of this site are useful. We never sell your details to anyone.

4. Who else sees it

The partners who help with a sale once it is under way, our email and analytics providers who act on our behalf, and regulators when the law requires it.

5. Cookies

One cookie remembers your theme choice. Analytics cookies load only after you accept them, and refusing them changes nothing about how the site works.

6. How long we keep it

Enquiries that go nowhere are kept for two years. Client files are kept for seven years after completion. Marketing consent lasts until you withdraw it.

7. Your rights

You can ask for a copy of what we hold, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Write to privacy@acre.homes and we will answer within thirty days.

8. Keeping it safe

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and access is limited to the people who need it. If a breach ever puts you at risk, we will tell you without delay.
