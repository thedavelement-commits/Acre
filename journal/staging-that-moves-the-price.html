Staging that actually moves the price

Most staging spend never comes back. Three things reliably do, and one of them costs sixty dollars.

Sellers spend thousands on staging every year, and most of it never shows up in the final price.

Light and the front door

Clean windows, warm bulbs and open curtains change how every room photographs. A freshly painted front door and one good plant set the tone before anyone steps inside.

The sixty dollar fix

Fix the dripping tap, oil the squeaky hinge and open a window an hour before each viewing. It costs about sixty dollars, and buyers always remember how a house felt.
