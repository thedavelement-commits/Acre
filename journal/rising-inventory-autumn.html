What rising inventory means for buyers this autumn

Inventory is up nineteen per cent. In two of our markets that means leverage. In the other two it mostly means the wrong homes are sitting.

For the first time in three years, buyers in our markets have more than a handful of homes to choose from. Inventory is up nineteen per cent on last autumn, and the headlines are calling it a buyer's market.

Why the reason matters

A rise caused by more sellers entering the market gives buyers real leverage. A rise caused by homes failing to sell at their asking price gives leverage only on those particular homes, and usually for a reason.

What we are telling buyers

Look at how long homes take to sell on your street, not across the whole city. In Ojai and Bend, good homes still move in under three weeks. In Asheville and Lake Oswego, patient buyers are negotiating real reductions.
